# ADR 005 — Authorised work and exact-version variations

**14 October 2026 · Local implementation · Chapters 09–10**

Work extends the selected contractor's immutable authorisation. Migration 008 adds a small current work projection, immutable effective terms, work events, proposal versions, decisions and deduplicated deadline events. Migrations 001–007 remain unchanged. Existing authorisations are backfilled from their exact selected quote/RFQ version, preserving the original scope, amount, actor and timestamp.

Use the existing transactional command service and shared transaction lock. Every consequential command checks current access, job version, exact proposal version, authorised terms and estate policy before committing facts, audit, tasks/outbox and receipt together. Retry returns the same outcome after rechecking authority. Optional progress evidence does not advance the decision version. This follows the existing bounded local architecture; production scoped locks and throughput testing remain release work. PostgreSQL documents the role of [transactional locking in consistency](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/applevel-consistency.html); request schemas follow [Fastify's validation guidance](https://fastify.dev/docs/latest/Reference/Validation-and-Serialization/).

Start records server-observed actual commencement separately from the accepted completion target. Known estimates require a complete future date/time with timezone. A temporary unknown estimate requires a nonblank reason and a separate +24 elapsed-hour submission deadline. Only Admin may extend that duty with a reason and future deadline. Estimate submissions/extensions and target revisions retain their history. A schedule never generates actual Start. G03 is now owner-approved: request a contractor start-date proposal, then exact CM/MCST acceptance, using existing T22 first-successful-notice 2/5-business-day reminders.

Scope/price, agreed-start and completion-date proposals share immutable version/confirmation/decision primitives. Only one proposal is pending per job, preventing competing bases and accidental lost updates. A proposing party can revise or withdraw through another named account with equal role authority; the assigned company confirms exact terms. Estate proposals need Contractor confirmation before a decision. Revisions invalidate prior confirmation and old tasks. Zero price impact is explicit; calculations use integer cents and signed bigint differences. No pending proposal authorises extra work.

CM approves at/below the estate threshold or within the last MCST-approved limit, as explicitly clarified by the owner on 14 October. CM reductions preserve that limit. Above-threshold totals beyond it require MCST; a new MCST amount becomes the last approved limit. MCST inherits CM authority, and logged Admin overrides require a reason. Reject with Price/Scope/Timing/Not needed/Other; Other needs an explanation. Withdrawal notes are optional. Outcomes append exact before/after terms and automatically resolve to W1/W2/R3/R1/E2 according to retained work context and Contractor-confirmed feasibility. Support or an unanswered proposal does not accept a target. Cancellation supersedes pending proposals and tasks; it cannot restore authority.

T20/T21/T23 use elapsed instants. Admin receives the initial missed completion/estimate/agreed-Start attention. Relevant CM and MCST receive the +24h completion/Start notice only while still outstanding. Copies describe missing app records rather than asserting physical non-performance. Accepted supersession, actual Start, completion or cancellation suppresses obsolete work notices. Immutable missed events retain effective due time and recording time; retries do not duplicate checkpoints. Okay on a lateness notice acknowledges only that notice. Intervention reuses contextual support and leaves the target unchanged.

Interruption pauses ordinary work. A reason/affected-work record creates or reuses an ordinary support case with Admin review. Only explicit Admin confirmation of safe and feasible retained scope resumes it. Unresolved verdicts require the actual next actor, action and future follow-up. They do not prove a repair, settle money or replace the contractor. Replacement retains the linked-new-ticket rule.

Estate users see their own work; only the assigned contractor company sees post-selection work terms, progress and files. Rivals are denied. Work files use the existing checked/chunked upload and private download paths. Masked CM projections conceal historical company/contact/person identifiers, actor identifiers and work evidence; raw contractor files remain withheld while masking is enabled. MCST/Admin retain records. Named attribution remains in immutable storage.

Chapter 11 must bind the combined invoice/completion declaration and physical completion occurrence, inspection rounds and affected acceptance invalidation to these terms/context primitives. Chapters 12–14 own final reconciliation, formal adjustments, payments, cancellation/settlement and full support execution. This ADR does not claim those integrations are implemented. Production providers, PostgreSQL deployment validation, native media queueing and security/load/operations checks retain their existing gates.
