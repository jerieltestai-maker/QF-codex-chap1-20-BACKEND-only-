# QuoteFix quotation field information icons

Working copy for the compulsory Yes/No declarations. Each information icon must open on click, tap, and keyboard focus. The two sentences under each field are the contractor-facing explanation of what Yes and No declare.

| Field label | Two-sentence information text |
| --- | --- |
| Waste disposal included | Yes means your final quoted amount covers collection, removal, and disposal of all waste generated by this job. No means waste disposal is excluded from your quote and must be arranged and paid for separately. |
| Warranty included | Yes means a warranty for the completed work is included in your final quoted amount. No means this quote offers no warranty for the completed work. |
| Making good included | Yes means your final quoted amount covers restoring every surface or fixture disturbed by your work to its prior condition. No means that restoration is excluded and must be arranged and paid for separately. |
| GST charged? | Yes means your final quoted amount includes every GST charge you will bill for this job. No means you declare that no GST will be charged for this job. |
| Access equipment included (shown only when the job is flagged) | Yes means your final quoted amount covers all access equipment needed to perform the work described in this RFQ. No means access equipment is excluded and must be arranged and paid for separately. |

The product owner confirmed **“GST charged?”** as the field label. In all cases, the contractor-entered amount is the final total payable by the MCST.

## Mandatory follow-ups — owner decision, 28 September 2026

The owner changed the previously optional follow-up details to mandatory responses when applicable, using predefined choices plus **Other** with an open response. Additional narrative terms and the contractor's PDF remain optional. The owner subsequently approved these option sets and schedule anchors. The owner approved a reasoned unknown-duration option on 2 October.

| Follow-up | When asked | Options / current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warranty duration | Warranty included = Yes | Approved: 7 days, 14 days, 1 month, 3 months, 1 year, Other (specify). Warranty = No does not ask for a fictitious duration. |
| Earliest availability | Every quote | Mandatory; approved choices relative to work authorisation and available site access: same day, next day, within 2–3 days, within 4–7 days, Other (specify). |
| Estimated work duration | Every quote | Mandatory; approved choices: up to half a day, 1 day, 2–3 days, 4–7 days, Other (specify). Also approved: **Unable to estimate yet — reason required**. Flag it prominently in comparison; it does not create a site-assessment readiness gate. |
| Exclusions | Every quote | Mandatory explicit response. Approved: carry forward services excluded by the Yes/No declarations, then allow additional predefined exclusions, Other, or “No additional exclusions.” Avoid re-entering the same fact or contradicting an earlier declaration. |

Missing required follow-ups cannot be accepted merely by calling the quote sparse. Optional detail can still be absent and visibly labelled “Not stated”; existing historical records must not have details fabricated. Unknown costs for excluded services must be explicit warnings in comparison and selection. The contractor's stated total and unknown third-party/excluded costs remain distinct.
