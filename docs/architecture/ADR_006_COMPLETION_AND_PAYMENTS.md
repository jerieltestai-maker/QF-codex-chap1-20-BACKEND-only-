## Decisions

Use the existing SQL transactions, current-role checks, version preconditions, operation receipts, immutable audit and durable notification outbox. Monetary values are integer SGD cents; totals use BigInt arithmetic. The bounded local monolith retains its shared transaction lock. Production concurrency/throughput still needs Chapter 19 validation.

Migration 009 adds immutable completion rounds, invoice versions, one inspection outcome per physical round, exact invoice confirmation and a unique billing source. Declaration time and contractor-declared physical completion time are separate. Store the completion target/version and physical lateness on the declaration event; inspection actor/date/result do not overwrite this provenance. A contractor declaration is not independent bank/work verification.

Every physically corrected resubmission creates a new round and clears operational acceptance. It links its originating inspected round and retains all findings. Invoice-only corrections retain acceptance when physical scope is unchanged. An approved scope change invalidates earlier acceptance and requires a new physical submission. An amount-only approved variation with unchanged scope retains acceptance. Reconciliation compares exact current authorised terms and invoice, rather than treating an explanation as approval. Invoice confirmation is MCST-only and freezes the exact version, amount, named actor and server time. Subsequent financial adjustments are Chapter 14 work.

The billing source preserves the Chapter 13 assessment trigger exactly once. G08 rounding/arithmetic remains open by the owner's explicit deferral; there are no placeholder calculated fees. G10 reputation questions remain separately deferred to Chapter 16, without obstructing operational acceptance.

Each payment transfer has immutable original source fields, checked completed-transfer proof and its own receipt state. Original proof object reuse is forbidden. Matching checksum/reference/amount-date context produces a possible-duplicate warning; explicit acknowledgment permits a genuinely separate instalment. It does not merge transfers. The actor confirms that the bank transfer completed; the app does not inspect a bank account or move money.

Successful durable issuance of the first authorised receipt notice records its timestamp and exact +7 elapsed-calendar-day due time in the same transaction. A failed/unsent notice produces no silence clock. Actual receipt keeps date-only or explicit-time precision, independently of report/notice timestamps. Payment Issue excludes that transfer from settlement and stops assumption. Assumption and user commands share the database lock and guarded state transition. A later issue retains assumed/actual history and opens contextual support. Admin can record verified actual receipt, void an invalid record or retain the unresolved dispute with a compulsory next action and future Admin follow-up. An unverified verdict does not fulfil the receipt obligation, close support, restart the original silence period or reuse expired disputed silence.

T08 inspection and T09 response follow-ups reuse the issued-notice 2/5-business-day mechanism. Invoice correction is T22, invoice confirmation T07. T01 +1/+3-business-day checkpoints stay anchored to original invoice confirmation; meaningful progress suppresses the missing-update +1 notice, preserves the remaining balance and dated follow-up, and does not reset +3. Receipt T02 is independent per transfer and has no invented 2/5-day reminder cadence.

Closure rechecks accepted current work, exact confirmed invoice, full actual/assumed settlement, active ordinary work and absence of linked open support obligations or pending variations. Fees and future Feedback do not block it. Zero payable uses the same confirmed performed-work outcome with no fictitious transfer. Cancellation stays Cancelled and awaits Chapter 14 settlement authority. Post-closure payment issues show Admin Intervention and retain the original closure event. Settlement anchors preserve actual/assumed precision for Chapter 15; correcting receipt provenance records an anchor revision/invalidation event.

## Integration and limits

Simple controls in the existing authorised-work panel expose completion, repeated inspections, invoice corrections, MCST confirmation, transfers, receipts, issues and verified Admin payment corrections. Declaration, positive work acceptance and Payment Sent use explicit slide controls. Review checkboxes start unchecked. Upload readiness and submission remain separate; uploads resume with current authority and matching checksums. Command recovery uses original operation identity. Load guards refuse mixed work/payment versions.

Assigned-company and estate access is checked before every command and recovered receipt. Unrelated contractors receive non-disclosing denials. Concealed CM identities redact contractor free text and document IDs; raw invoice/work/payment proof downloads are withheld. Named estate decisions remain attributable. Billing sources are internal and absent from CM projections.

Chapter 13 owns calculated fees, collection/restrictions and adjustments. Chapter 14 owns comprehensive case verdict/execution, cancellation and amended settlements; current payment-specific execution evidence and open-obligation guards are integration foundations. Chapter 15 owns Feedback dispatch/forms and anchor amendments after feedback issuance. G14 retention, G04 warranty automation, providers/private object storage and production verification remain unchanged. Test scan adapters are confined to disposable fictional tests; the normal application scanner still fails closed.
