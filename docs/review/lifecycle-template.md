**Job** means the real-world maintenance work; **ticket** means its QuoteFix record/ID. Use Job in normal UI and Ticket ID in references; these are not two competing workspaces. The **RFQ** is the brief and quotation process within that record. A nomination, quote round and work authorisation are separate recorded events. A rejected nominee need not mean the maintenance need was cancelled.

CM or MCST creates/publishes an RFQ. AI structures observed facts without inventing specifications/diagnoses, then helps the human review clarity/privacy before publication. Preserve originals and manual route. Grouping stays with the author; combined RFQ receives whole-bundle quotes from one accountable contractor with disclosed suitable subcontracting. Controlled shared factual Q&A is available before a first quote; site visits still need an initial quote. Unknown quote duration requires a reason and a prominent comparison caution.

Only verified eligible contractors quote. RFQs do not expire. Quote validity is checked at selection; selected scope/price held through required MCST approval with the approved contractor checkbox. ## Admin powers and backtracking

4. **D4 — final resolution:** Admin issues the settled outcome, or a separately attributable binding verdict through D5. Relevant participants have **Acknowledge resolution** only; no in-app rejection/counterproposal route. Record each acknowledgment against the exact resolution version. Already-authorised actions proceed in parallel: acknowledgment does not gate repair, payment or verified case resolution.
